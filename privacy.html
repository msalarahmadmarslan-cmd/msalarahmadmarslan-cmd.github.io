Privacy Policy

App: General Knowledge (GK Quiz)
Last updated: 29 October 2026

"General Knowledge" (the "app") is a quiz game created by Rabia. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information the app uses

  • Player name: the name you type before playing. It is shown on the public leaderboard, so please do not use your full real name or any private information.
  • Quiz scores: your total points, your best round, the number of rounds you have played, and the time of your last play.
  • Anonymous ID: the app creates a random anonymous ID (using Firebase Anonymous Authentication) so that each player can update only their own leaderboard entry. It is not linked to your email, phone number or identity.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location. It has no advertisements and no in-app purchases, and it does not use analytics or tracking tools.

How the information is used

Your name and scores are used only to show the leaderboard and your own statistics. The leaderboard is public, so other players can see names and scores.

Where the information is stored

Leaderboard data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Firebase is provided by Google LLC and processes the data on our behalf. More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Questions read aloud

The app can read questions aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. No voice is recorded.

Children

The app is a general-knowledge game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the name they choose to type. If you are a parent or guardian and want an entry removed, please contact us.

Retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept until they are deleted. To request deletion of your entry, email us at the address below with the name you used, and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
